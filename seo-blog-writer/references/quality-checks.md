# Article quality checks

Review the actual article and its destination contract. Do not award a score for keyword density, length, FAQ count, or link count. A polished sounding article with unsupported claims fails.

## Blocking checks

- **Accuracy:** Trace consequential claims, prices, comparisons, numbers, and direct quotes to sources that support the exact wording and scope. Check the source date, plan, locale, sample, and relevant caveats. Remove or qualify unsupported claims; hedging must not disguise an unknown fact.
- **Links:** Open or otherwise verify each cited destination and relevant internal link when tools allow. Confirm the linked page actually supports the nearby claim. A fetch error, paywall, or blocked tool is “unverified,” not proof of a 404; find another source or state the limitation.
- **Attribution and honesty:** Do not imply testing, original research, an author, an update date, firsthand use, or measured results that did not occur. Separate reporting from editorial opinion. Respect source and image permissions.
- **Required format:** Check the user/site schema, locale, slug, Markdown/frontmatter, visible metadata, and image rules. For a WebP destination, run the image gate in [drafting](drafting.md).

## Editorial checks

Read the page from top to bottom, preferably aloud in the target language.

- **Reader value:** Can the intended reader find the promised answer and decide what to do? For comparisons, are criteria explicit, meaningful exclusions and head-to-head differences visible where the evidence supports them, and recommendations conditional on real needs? Are examples useful, limits visible, and repeated sections removed?
- **Whole-article voice:** Does the opening set up the same article the body delivers? Do sections build on each other? Are transitions earned, recommendations explained, and the ending specific? Replace boilerplate and repetitive paragraph shapes.
- **English:** Does it sound natural for this publication and genre? Check inflated adjectives, generic phrases, sudden register changes, awkward keyword placement, and claims of experience.
- **Chinese:** Does it read as original writing to a native reader? Check literal English syntax, unnatural terminology, stiff transitions, slogan-like abstractions, simplified/traditional usage, and locally inaccurate examples. Compare facts and qualifiers with the source, not sentence order.
- **Search usefulness:** Title, H1, description, headings, and links should describe the real content in the reader's terms. No forced exact-match phrases or sections written solely for an imagined citation algorithm. Add an FAQ only if it answers distinct questions the body does not.
- **Presentation:** Scan on mobile-sized paragraphs and tables, meaningful image alt text, clear headings, and CTA relevance where the destination supports them.

Fix the concrete issues, then recheck the changed passages and their joins. For complex articles, optionally use `$seo-geo-qa` as a separate review, but this writer remains responsible for its own final pass.

## Concise handoff

When a report is useful, record:
- verdict: ready for review / needs revision, with reasons;
- sources and links checked, plus any that remain unverified;
- one or two substantive editorial decisions (especially the English voice or Chinese localization choices);
- destination-format and WebP result where applicable;
- remaining factual or access limitations.

Do not make ranking, traffic, citation, or publication claims from this check.
